Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What's the minimum budget to get started? +

Most clients start with a monthly ad spend of ৳20,000–৳50,000 alongside our management fee. We'll tell you honestly if your budget is too small for a channel to work well.

Is there a minimum contract period? +

We ask for a 3-month minimum, since ad accounts need time to leave the learning phase. After that, it's month-to-month.

How often will I get reports? +

Starter plans receive bi-weekly reports; Growth and Scale plans get weekly reports plus a live strategy call.

Can you help with payment gateway setup too? +

Yes — payment integration is one of our core services, and it's included from the Growth plan upward.

Do you work with businesses outside Bangladesh? +

Yes — we work with clients across Bangladesh and worldwide, with everything coordinated remotely over call, email and WhatsApp.
